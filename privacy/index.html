Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

The Simple Version

We analyze your public social media to create your food match report. We collect your name, email, and social profile link. We use Paddle for payments (we never see your card). We delete everything after 30 days. We don't sell your data.

What Information We Collect

Information You Give Us:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your public Instagram/Facebook/social media profile link

Information We Automatically Collect:

  • Order ID (we generate this)
  • IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Date and time of your order

Information We DON'T Collect:

  • Credit card numbers (PayPal handles payments)
  • Private messages or DMs
  • Your photos or face (we only analyze post captions and patterns)
  • Any biometric or facial recognition data

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  1. Provide the service: Analyze your public social media patterns
  2. Deliver your report: Send results to your email
  3. Process payment: Via Paddle (we don't store payment info)
  4. Improve our service: Understand how people use YourMealMatch
  5. Legal compliance: If required by law

We will NEVER:

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Share your email with marketers
  • Post on your social media
  • Access your private content
  • Track you across other websites

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Order information: 30 days, then automatically deleted
  • Your report: We don't keep a copy after emailing it to you
  • Email address: Deleted after 30 days unless you subscribe to updates (optional)

You can request immediate deletion by contacting us at shiref.abouzaid@gmail.com

Third-Party Services

We use these trusted services:

Paddle (Payment Processing - Merchant of Record)

  • Handles all payments securely as our Merchant of Record
  • Processes billing, receipts, and tax collection
  • We never see your credit card details
  • Paddle Privacy Policy

Cloudflare (Hosting & Database)

  • Stores order data temporarily (30 days)
  • Industry-standard security
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • Essential cookies: To remember your order during checkout
  • Analytics cookies: To understand how people use the site (if you consent)

You can disable cookies in your browser, but the checkout may not work properly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Request a copy of what we have
  • Delete your data: Request immediate deletion
  • Correct your data: Update incorrect information
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from any emails
  • Object: Ask us to stop processing your data

To exercise these rights, email us at: shiref.abouzaid@gmail.com

Children's Privacy

YourMealMatch is not for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has used our service, please contact us immediately.

International Users

We operate globally. If you're outside your country:

  • Your data may be transferred to our servers
  • We comply with GDPR (Europe) and CCPA (California)
  • We protect your data regardless of location

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

For significant changes, we'll email you (if we have your email).
