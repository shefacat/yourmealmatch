Refund Policy

Last updated: January 8, 2026

The Simple Version

We process refunds on a case-by-case basis within 14 days of purchase. Contact us at shiref.abouzaid@gmail.com with your order details to request a refund. Approved refunds are typically processed within 5-10 business days.

1. Refund Policy

We process refunds on a case-by-case basis within 14 days of purchase.

Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of your purchase date. Each request is reviewed individually and processed at our discretion.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at shiref.abouzaid@gmail.com with:

  • Your order number
  • Email address used for purchase
  • Brief reason for your request

3. Refund Processing

Refund requests are reviewed individually. Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days to your original payment method.

You will receive email confirmation once your refund has been processed.

4. Eligibility

Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of purchase. Digital products that have been fully delivered and accessed may have limited refund eligibility, except where required by applicable consumer protection laws.

This refund policy does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer under local law.

5. Didn't Receive Your Report?

If you haven't received your report within 48 hours, please try these steps before requesting a refund:

  1. Check your spam/junk folder - Our emails sometimes get filtered
  2. Search your inbox for emails from YourMealMatch
  3. Verify your email address - Check that you entered it correctly during purchase
  4. Wait 48 hours - Reports are delivered within 24-48 hours
  5. Contact us at shiref.abouzaid@gmail.com - We'll investigate and resend if needed

Response time: We respond to all emails within 48 hours.

6. Payment Processing (Paddle)

All payments are processed by Paddle, our authorized payment partner and Merchant of Record.

This means:

  • Your receipt and invoice come from Paddle
  • Refunds are processed through Paddle
  • Paddle handles all billing and payment disputes

For payment-related questions, you can also contact Paddle support: https://www.paddle.com/support

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Your purchase is governed by the Refund Policy in effect at the time of purchase.
